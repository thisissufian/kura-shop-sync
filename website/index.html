Decentralized Sync

"Brave-Style" Sync Chain

Pair Owner & Cashier devices via QR code or 12-word mnemonic. Zero cloud server required; phones discover each other over UDP 42851 and sync deltas over TCP 42850.

Commutative CRDT Ledger
• Integer minor units (i64)

Hardware Barcode Gun & Vision

<35ms Laser Gun Detection & Native Camera Scanner

Plug in any USB or Bluetooth laser barcode gun. Kura intercepts sub-35ms keystroke bursts and looks up items or past receipts instantly without losing search focus. Need mobile scanning? The native BarcodeDetector camera scans EAN-13, UPC-A, Code-128, and Code-39 with zero third-party libraries.
